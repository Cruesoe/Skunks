[b]Adds wild skunks to the rim: small, smelly, and ready to ruin someone's day.[/b]

This is a continued version of the original mod by GuppyFacesAreCute. It will be removed from the Workshop if the original author requests it.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Wild skunks live in temperate forests, temperate swamps, boreal forests and arid shrublands, and in Nature's Pretty Sweet redwood forests and ReGrowth aspen forests when those mods are loaded.
[*]Skunks are common trade animals: small omnivores that aren't trainable and make poor pets.
[*]Butcher them for regular small-animal meat and dark skunkskin leather.
[/list]

[h2]Spraying[/h2]
[list]
[*]Hurt a skunk up close and it turns around and sprays a narrow jet of musk at whoever hit it.
[*]Predators leave skunks alone, because animals know better than to mess with one.
[*]Each skunk has 5 sprays with a short cooldown between them, and the charges refill over about 3 days.
[*]Destroying its anus or stink glands stops it from spraying for good.
[*]While it still has sprays left it runs away; once it's empty it stops fleeing and fights back.
[*]Ranged attacks won't make a skunk spray, but they still scare it off.
[/list]

[h2]Skunk musk[/h2]
[list]
[*]Anyone standing in the musk cloud is covered in skunk musk for about 5 days, which gives −8 mood, and anyone within 3 tiles of them gets −6 mood.
[*]An hour outside makes it wear off 10% faster until you go indoors, and an hour indoors makes it wear off 10% slower until you go outside.
[*]Colonists hooked on smokeleaf take only half those mood hits.
[*]Full toxic-environment resistance blocks the coating, and clears it faster if you already have it.
[*]Animals caught in the spray are stunned for 3 seconds, and skunks are immune to their own musk.
[/list]

[h2]Cleaning it off[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Neutroamine (1)[/b]: removes it instantly.
[*][b]Chemfuel (1)[/b]: removes it instantly, but has a 50% chance to cause 2–4 burns (never on the head).
[*][b]Herbal medicine (5)[/b] or [b]milk (5)[/b]: clears it over about 24 hours.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Works with Nature's Pretty Sweet and ReGrowth: Aspen Forest, adding skunks to their biomes.
Safe to add to an existing save.
