# Skunks - Continued

Adds wild skunks to temperate forests, swamps, boreal forests and arid shrublands. Hurt one up close and it sprays musk that coats anyone nearby, souring their mood and the mood of everyone around them until it wears off or is washed away with neutroamine, chemfuel, herbal medicine or milk. Predators leave skunks alone, and they flee while they still have spray left.

A continued version of the original mod by GuppyFacesAreCute.
